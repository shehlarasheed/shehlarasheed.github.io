About Shehla

A human lens on
psychological science.

I am a psychologist, counsellor and researcher working across clinical practice, mental health research, psychometrics, neurodevelopment and community-based research.

Psychology is most meaningful when evidence remains connected to people, context and lived experience.

My professional experience spans clinical psychology, counselling, psychological assessment, research, evidence synthesis and public communication.

I hold an MPhil in Psychology from Fatima Jinnah Women University and a BS in Psychology from International Islamic University Islamabad. My MPhil research examined mental health problems, post-migration living difficulties and resilience among Afghan refugees.

Alongside research, I have worked with children and adults with neurodevelopmental and mental health conditions and have provided trauma-informed psychosocial support in community rehabilitation settings.
