About Me

Psychology · Research · Practice

Clinical experience, interdisciplinary research, psychometrics, behavioral science, and professional communication.

Connecting psychological research with real-world practice.

I am a psychology professional and researcher with over seven years of combined clinical and research experience. My work has developed across academic, clinical, community, and institutional settings.

My experience includes psychological research, psychometric research, mixed-methods methodology, clinical assessment and intervention, behavioral science, evidence synthesis, analytical writing, and research communication.

I am particularly interested in understanding psychological well-being, motivation, resilience, adaptation, executive functioning, affective regulation, and human behaviour across diverse populations and contexts.
